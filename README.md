# अशब्द (ashabd.in)

> **अशब्द** — An antique manuscript experience.

A minimalist, atmospheric, centuries-old aged parchment page bearing strictly the official sacred identity **अशब्द / ASHABD**.

---

## 📜 Aesthetic Highlights

- **Official Ashabd Logo Centerpiece**: The authentic brand logo from `ashabd_website_logo_pack` (`logo_transparent_1000w.webp` / `.png`) featuring the Devanagari calligraphy “अशब्द”, the fountain pen and feather quill emblem, and the understated “ASHABD” typography underneath.
- **Natural Aged Ink Integration**: Seamlessly blended into antique parchment fibers using `mix-blend-mode: multiply`, subtle gall-ink toning, microscopic ink soak, and gentle transparency (no artificial digital sticker/PNG look).
- **Ancient Weathered Parchment**: Procedurally generated organic paper canvas with water tide-lines, drying creases, cellulose fibers, foxing oxidation spots, and scorched corner vignettes.
- **Understated Ink Marginalia**: Monochromatic, subtle social links for `@ashabd0` on Instagram and X (Twitter) at the foot of the parchment.
- **Pure Minimalism**: Zero navigation bars, zero menus, zero cards, zero scrollbars (`100vw × 100vh`). Centered in silent, meditative stillness.
- **SEO & Social Preview**: Integrated Open Graph banners (`og-banner-1200x630.jpg`) and multi-resolution favicons for mobile and desktop.
